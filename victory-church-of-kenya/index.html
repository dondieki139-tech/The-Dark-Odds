Who We Are

A church family rooted in faith and compassion

Victory Church of Kenya & Orphanage is a developing church community in Kisii, committed to sharing the hope of Jesus Christ, building a caring fellowship and serving people in need. We are still growing and do not yet have a stable, permanent church building or consistent worship facility. We continue to gather and serve as resources allow, trusting God and welcoming people who would like to stand with us as the ministry develops.

Our Heart

Faith that reaches beyond the church walls

We believe the love of God should be seen in how we care for one another. Through prayer, teaching, fellowship and community outreach, we seek to make a lasting difference in the lives of families and children.

Our Current Challenges

15 needs facing our developing church

As a growing church in Kisii, we are working to strengthen our worship, teaching, fellowship and outreach. We do not yet have a stable church building. These are practical areas where prayer, donated items, volunteers and financial support could help us grow.

01
No stable church building or worship venueWe need a dependable, safe place where the congregation can gather regularly for worship, prayer and fellowship.
02
Shortage of BiblesWe need Bibles for members, new believers, families and people who want to study God’s Word.
03
Limited chairs and seatingWe need enough chairs or benches so worshippers, elderly members and visitors can sit comfortably.
04
Lack of a pulpit and church furnitureA pulpit, tables and basic furniture would support orderly services and ministry activities.
05
Shortage of sound equipmentMicrophones, speakers and a mixer would help the congregation hear preaching, prayers and worship clearly.
06
Limited musical instrumentsBasic instruments would help the worship team lead congregational singing.
07
No dependable electricity and lighting setupWe need safe lighting and reliable power arrangements for gatherings and evening activities where needed.
08
Limited Sunday school materialsChildren need age-appropriate Bible lessons, storybooks, teaching aids and activity materials.
09
Shortage of discipleship and Bible study resourcesStudy guides, notebooks and teaching materials would strengthen spiritual growth and understanding.
10
Insufficient funds for venue and basic operating costsRegular resources are needed for meeting space, cleaning, communication and essential ministry expenses.
11
Limited storage and secure equipment spaceA safe place to keep Bibles, instruments, chairs and other donated church items would protect them.
12
Inadequate sanitation and handwashing facilitiesClean toilets, handwashing points and cleaning supplies are important for a healthy gathering place.
13
Limited transport for outreach and ministry visitsTransport assistance would help leaders and volunteers reach families, members and people needing support.
14
Insufficient resources for community outreachWe need materials and practical support to visit, encourage and assist vulnerable people in our community.
15
Limited capacity to support ministry volunteers and leadershipTraining, teaching resources and basic ministry support would help volunteers serve effectively and sustain the church’s growth.

A Place of Care

Our Orphanage: giving children hope for tomorrow

Alongside our church ministry, Victory Church of Kenya & Orphanage cares for orphaned and vulnerable children. Our desire is to provide a safe, loving environment where every child is valued, supported and encouraged to reach their potential.

Support can help provide daily meals, clothing, school needs, healthcare and a caring home. Every act of kindness makes a meaningful difference.

Child Wellbeing & Stewardship

Care with dignity, responsibility and respect

Children’s safety and wellbeing must always come first. Our ministry is committed to treating every child with dignity, protecting their privacy, listening to their needs and ensuring that support is used for its stated purpose.

Child safety and dignity

Children should be treated respectfully and protected from abuse, neglect, exploitation and discrimination. Their personal stories, images and information should only be shared with appropriate consent and care.

Responsible use of gifts

Donations should be recorded, handled carefully and used for the needs communicated to supporters. Donors may contact the church to ask questions about a contribution.

Open communication

We welcome questions, prayer, constructive feedback and conversations about how to support the children and the wider community.

Our promise to supporters

We will aim to communicate honestly about needs and activities, avoid exaggerated claims, respect children’s privacy and share genuine updates when available. We do not publish invented numbers, impact statistics, partner names or testimonials.

Be part of the difference

Pray with us, volunteer your time, partner with the ministry or support the needs of the children in our care. Together, we can share hope and show God’s love in action.
