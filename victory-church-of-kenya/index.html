Who We Are

A church family rooted in faith and compassion

Victory Church of Kenya & Orphanage is a developing church community in Kisii South, Bigirango, committed to sharing the hope of Jesus Christ, building a caring fellowship and serving people in need. We are still growing and do not yet have a stable, permanent church building or consistent worship facility. We continue to gather and serve as resources allow, trusting God and welcoming people who would like to stand with us as the ministry develops.

Our Heart

Faith that reaches beyond the church walls

We believe the love of God should be seen in how we care for one another. Through prayer, teaching, fellowship and community outreach, we seek to make a lasting difference in the lives of families and children.

Our Current Challenges

15 major challenges facing our developing church

Victory Church of Kenya is a developing church community in Kisii. We are committed to worship, biblical teaching, fellowship and community service, but limited facilities and resources make this work difficult. We do not yet have a stable, permanent church building. These are the practical challenges where prayer, donated items, volunteers and support can help us grow.

01
Lack of a stable church buildingWe do not yet have our own permanent church building or a dependable worship venue. This makes it difficult to plan regular services and provide a consistent place for worship, prayer, fellowship and church activities.
02
Shortage of Holy BiblesWe do not have enough Bibles for members, new believers and families. Donated Bibles would help people read Scripture, participate in Bible study and grow in their faith.
03
Insufficient chairs and seatingWe need more chairs and benches so members, elderly worshippers, children and visitors can sit safely and comfortably during services and meetings.
04
Lack of a pulpit and essential church furnitureA pulpit, tables, cupboards and other basic furniture would help us organise services, teaching sessions and ministry activities.
05
Shortage of sound equipmentMicrophones, speakers, a mixer and related equipment would help everyone hear preaching, prayers, announcements and worship clearly.
06
Lack of musical instrumentsKeyboards, drums, guitars and other suitable instruments would support the worship team and encourage congregational praise and singing.
07
Limited electricity and lightingWe need safe, dependable lighting and suitable power arrangements for worship, meetings and evening activities.
08
Insufficient Sunday school materialsChildren need age-appropriate Bibles, lesson books, teaching charts, stationery and activity materials to learn about the Christian faith.
09
Lack of Bible study and discipleship resourcesStudy guides, Christian books, notebooks and teaching materials would help members understand Scripture and develop their faith.
10
Financial challenges in running church activitiesLimited funds make it difficult to meet venue costs, cleaning, communication, ministry supplies and other essential operating expenses.
11
Lack of secure storage facilitiesWe need cupboards, shelves and a secure storage area to protect Bibles, instruments, chairs, documents and donated equipment.
12
Inadequate sanitation facilitiesClean toilets, handwashing facilities, water storage and cleaning supplies are important for the health and dignity of everyone attending gatherings.
13
Lack of reliable transportationTransport costs make it difficult to organise evangelism, visit families, reach members and assist people who need ministry support.
14
Limited evangelism and community outreach resourcesWe need Bibles, Christian literature, transport and practical supplies to share the Gospel and serve vulnerable people in our community.
15
Limited resources for church workers and volunteersTraining, teaching materials and basic ministry resources would help church leaders and volunteers serve effectively and sustain the growth of the congregation.

Our Children

Supporting 43 orphaned children in Kisii South, Bigirango

Our ministry currently cares for 43 orphaned children, aged 7 to 18. We seek to support their daily needs, education, health, safety, emotional wellbeing and spiritual growth. Every child deserves dignity, encouragement and the opportunity to build a hopeful future.

A Place of Care

Our Orphanage: giving children hope for tomorrow

Alongside our church ministry, Victory Church of Kenya & Orphanage cares for orphaned and vulnerable children. Our desire is to provide a safe, loving environment where every child is valued, supported and encouraged to reach their potential.

Support can help provide daily meals, clothing, school needs, healthcare and a caring home. Every act of kindness makes a meaningful difference.

Children’s age range: 7–18 years. Number currently supported: 43 children.

Safety: The church reports that two security guards are present to help maintain safety at the children’s premises. Security personnel are one part of safety; appropriate supervision, child-protection procedures and safe reporting arrangements remain important too.

Practical Care for Children

18 important needs at our orphanage

Children need more than a roof over their heads. Consistent support with food, safe accommodation, health, education, hygiene, emotional wellbeing and spiritual growth helps them develop with dignity and hope. These are key areas where donations and practical assistance can make a difference.

01
Food and daily nutritious mealsMaize flour, rice, beans, cooking oil, vegetables, fruits and other food supplies are needed to prepare balanced meals and help children grow healthily.
02
Clean and safe drinking waterA reliable supply of clean water is essential for drinking, cooking, washing and everyday hygiene.
03
Clothing and shoesChildren need suitable everyday clothes, sweaters, socks, shoes and sandals that fit their ages, sizes and changing needs.
04
School fees and education expensesSupport is needed for school fees, examination costs and other essential education expenses so children can continue learning.
05
School uniformsUniforms, sweaters, ties, socks, school shoes and sportswear help children attend school comfortably and confidently.
06
Books and stationeryExercise books, pens, pencils, mathematical sets, school bags, textbooks and other learning materials support children's education.
07
Holy Bibles and Christian learning materialsChildren need age-appropriate Bibles, Bible storybooks, Sunday school materials and Christian teaching resources for spiritual growth.
08
Medical care and prescribed medicinesSupport is needed for clinic visits, medical check-ups, treatment and prescribed medicines when children are unwell.
09
Bedding, mattresses and blanketsMattresses, bedsheets, blankets, pillows and mosquito nets help provide clean, comfortable and safer sleeping conditions.
10
Beds and adequate sleeping facilitiesProper beds and sufficient sleeping space are important so each child has a decent and comfortable place to rest.
11
Soap and personal hygiene productsBathing soap, washing powder, toothpaste, toothbrushes, sanitary towels and tissue paper help children maintain personal hygiene.
12
Cooking equipment and kitchen suppliesCooking pots, plates, cups, spoons, serving utensils and safe cooking fuel are needed to prepare and serve daily meals.
13
Sanitation and cleaning suppliesDisinfectants, detergents, brooms, mops, buckets, toilet-cleaning products and waste-disposal supplies help maintain a clean environment.
14
Safe and stable accommodationSupport is needed to improve the children's living environment and provide secure, dependable and suitable accommodation.
15
Furniture and storage facilitiesTables, chairs, cupboards, wardrobes and shelves help keep children's belongings, clothes, books and supplies organised.
16
Educational, recreational and sports activitiesBalls, toys, drawing materials, games, sports equipment and learning activities encourage creativity, exercise, confidence and teamwork.
17
Emotional, social and mentorship supportChildren need patient care, encouragement, positive role models, guidance and access to appropriate counselling support when needed.
18
Transport and emergency assistanceHelp with transport to school, hospitals and important appointments, as well as urgent unexpected needs, supports children's safety and wellbeing.

How support is used

Support can be directed towards one or more of these needs. We encourage donors to state the purpose of their contribution and keep their transaction confirmation. The ministry should maintain clear records of donations received and how assistance is used, and provide updates to supporters.

Child Wellbeing & Stewardship

Care with dignity, responsibility and respect

Children’s safety and wellbeing must always come first. Our ministry is committed to treating every child with dignity, protecting their privacy, listening to their needs and ensuring that support is used for its stated purpose.

Child safety and dignity

Children should be treated respectfully and protected from abuse, neglect, exploitation and discrimination. Their personal stories, images and information should only be shared with appropriate consent and care.

Responsible use of gifts

Donations should be recorded, handled carefully and used for the needs communicated to supporters. Donors may contact the church to ask questions about a contribution.

Open communication

We welcome questions, prayer, constructive feedback and conversations about how to support the children and the wider community.

Our promise to supporters

We will aim to communicate honestly about needs and activities, avoid exaggerated claims, respect children’s privacy and share genuine updates when available. We do not publish invented numbers, impact statistics, partner names or testimonials.

Your Information Matters

Privacy Policy

We respect the privacy and dignity of visitors, supporters, church members and the children in our care. This notice explains how information shared with Victory Church of Kenya & Orphanage may be used.

1. Information we receive

If you contact us by email, phone or WhatsApp, we may receive your name, contact details and the message or enquiry you choose to share. If you make an M-Pesa contribution, the payment is processed by Safaricom and the relevant transaction details may be visible to the sender and recipient.

2. How we use information

We use information to respond to enquiries, communicate about church activities, acknowledge donations, discuss support for stated needs and keep appropriate ministry records. We should only request information that is relevant to the purpose.

3. Children’s privacy

We do not intend this website to collect personal information directly from children. We should not publish a child’s full name, personal history, school details, contact information or identifiable photograph without appropriate permission from a parent or lawful guardian and consideration of the child’s best interests.

4. Sharing information

We do not sell personal information. We should only share it when necessary to provide a requested service, process a payment through the relevant provider, meet a legal obligation or protect someone’s safety. Messaging and payment providers have their own privacy practices.

5. Keeping information secure

We aim to limit access to personal information to people who need it for ministry administration, keep records appropriately and take reasonable steps to prevent loss, misuse or unauthorised access. No online communication or storage method can be guaranteed completely secure.

6. Retention and your choices

We should keep information only for as long as it is reasonably needed for the purpose, ministry records or applicable legal requirements. You may contact us to ask what personal information we hold or request correction or deletion where appropriate and lawful.

Contact about privacy

For privacy questions or requests, email makorib788@gmail.com or call/WhatsApp +254 746 771 677. Please do not send sensitive information about a child through a public comment or an unsecured channel.

Important: This is a plain-language privacy notice for the website, not a legal compliance certification. The church should review its actual record-keeping and practices, appoint a responsible person to handle privacy requests, and obtain appropriate consent before collecting or publishing personal information—especially children’s information.
